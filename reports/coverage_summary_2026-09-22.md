## Main dangers across the book

Crowding at highs. 49 quality names and 46 cyclicals are priced for perfection or at a cycle peak — 42% of coverage. The largest single risk remains buying good companies at the top of their ranges.

The AI trade, both sides. The AI-fear basket (ADBE, NOW, SAP, WKL, INTU, ACN) and the AI-capex winners (NVDA, AMD, MRVL, AVGO, PLTR, VST, CEG, TT, ETN) are mirror images of one debate. A capex digestion phase hits them in opposite directions; a software-sector selloff like this week's hits the basket regardless of the numbers.

Cheap-for-a-reason. Cable (CMCSA, CHTR), California utilities (EIX, PCG), Fiserv and Nike screen cheapest in coverage; in every case the E in the floor is contested. They are labelled, not promoted.

Rate sensitivity on the buy side. PEG, SCHW, TMUS and the regulated names on the buy list all lose if long rates keep rising; the EU banks on the sell list lose if they fall. The book is not neutral to the rate path.
