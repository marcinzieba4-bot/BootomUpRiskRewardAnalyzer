## How the classification works (read `scripts/quality_classify.py` first)
Each ticker gets a class = business durability × price.
- Durability (axis 1) is YOUR call, stored per ticker in `data/business_profiles.json`: `kind` ∈ DURABLE | CYCLICAL | AI_EXPOSED | STRUCTURAL | TURNAROUND | SPECIAL, `risk` = obsolescence risk 1 (very low) … 5 (existential), `why` = one sentence, `reviewed` = date.
  - DURABLE: franchise/compounder, low risk the business becomes useless.
  - CYCLICAL: good business whose earnings ARE the cycle (commodities, semis, autos, cap goods, banks, refiners, airlines/lodging when demand-cyclical).
  - AI_EXPOSED: a franchise whose core product faces a live generative-AI / autonomous-AI substitution debate (software seats, IT services, professional information, search, mobility). Use this whenever a cheap software or services name is cheap BECAUSE the market prices AI disruption — do not file those under DURABLE.
  - STRUCTURAL: secular decline or a live existential threat outside AI (cable broadband, cigarettes, patent cliffs without a pipeline).
  - TURNAROUND: franchise not permanently impaired but earnings currently broken (the EPP floor is unreliable).
  - SPECIAL: deal arbitrage, binary legal/legislative outcome, a bond.
- Price (axis 2: CHEAP / FAIR / RICH) is computed deterministically by the script from the model's own numbers (Ratio B, distance to the EPP panic floor, adjusted-vs-market composite gap, conservative 2-yr case). Do not hand-edit it.
