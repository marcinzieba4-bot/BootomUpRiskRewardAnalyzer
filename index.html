Bottom-Up Risk/Reward Analyzer

Quantitative scenario modeling for individual equities

Data-driven scenario probabilities
for equity investing

A Python framework that combines fundamental analysis, proxy/alt-data signals, and structural factors to score stocks across Bear, Base, Bull, and Extreme-Bull scenarios.

Python 3 15 Tickers Covered Signal Models Proxy Models Scenario Weighting

Methodology

📊

Signal Models

Weighted leading indicators per ticker — hyperscaler CapEx, peer revenue trends, macro proxies — scored against bear/base/bull/extreme-bull thresholds.

👥

Proxy & Alt-Data Model

External data sources (supply-chain peers, channel checks, TSMC mix data) that lead the subject company's own reported results by 1–2 quarters.

⚖

Structural Factors

Qualitative moat adjustments — switching costs, management quality, regulatory risk, concentration risk — applied as score modifiers.

⚖

Blended Verdict

Fundamental and proxy scores are blended (default 60/40), converting the composite into scenario probabilities and a probability-weighted EV.

Scenario Framework

Bull Accelerating demand, margin expansion, positive surprises 2.0 – 3.0
